# Rebel Tech World: THE REBEL GRID

Owner-directed design revision, 2026-10-27. Supersedes the earlier tropical, grassy and cyan-accent concept. This is a construction specification, NOT a claim that the new rides or scenery are already installed.

## Mandatory visual direction

- Dark technology city / computer-grid environment, inspired by the visual language of TRON but branded Rebel Tech rather than copying Disney logos.
- NO visible grass, planted lawns, tropical palms, flower beds or naturalistic greenery inside the finished buildable park. Use charcoal terrain, black paving, basalt, metal decking, gridded plazas and dark perimeter screening. Distant terrain outside the editable park must be screened where practical, not falsely claimed editable.
- Main palette: obsidian #090B10; midnight navy #061A33; signal red #E10616; saturated electric BLUE #244CFF; pure white #FFFFFF. Blue must not drift into cyan/teal; red replaces orange.
- Street/path/entrance/task lights must be RGB 255,255,255 white, not warm yellow, amber or incandescent. Red and blue are architectural accents, not the primary walking-surface light.
- Darkness is art direction, not unreadable paths: give paths, steps, exits, signs and queue entrances adequate white light.
- Reuse large paving/roof/wall modules; do not carpet the entire park in tiny scenery tiles. Instance repeated props and reserve real lights for useful illumination; emissive trims elsewhere. Raven's performance must be measured rather than assumed.
- The current placed stock rides are prototypes. Default Viking, tropical and shop branding must be replaced/hidden before those areas count as finished.

## Three matching mobile-workshop vans

Use one reusable high-roof long-wheelbase van model/blueprint with three staged instances, not three heavy unique meshes.
1. BOOT BAY: main gate / information plaza. Closed clean display van, roof rack and white scene lights.
2. FIELD SERVICE 02: Clanka Labs. Open-side workshop pose, retractable awning, bench, tool cases, ladder and networking equipment represented economically.
3. NETWORK OPS 03: Cooling Loop / backstage edge. Service pose with roof terminal, black wheels and matching red/navy/white wrap.

Match the supplied reference silhouette, high roof, long body, roof rack/solar-terminal equipment, awning, diagonal wrap and RT branding. An original simplified prototype is acceptable for iteration but must not be described as an exact finished replica. Do not copy malformed tiny AI-generated text from the van reference. Keep door/counter access and guest paths clear.

## Every guest building gets a Rebel Tech shell

Functional PC2 shops/toilets/restaurants remain inside custom original exteriors. Custom appearance must not break their working doorway/counter or paths.
- BYTE BITE: food court / existing hotdog and sushi counters.
- OVERCLOCK CAFE: drinks and coffee.
- REBOOT RESTROOMS: toilets; conspicuous readable white signs.
- CACHE & CARRY: retail / souvenirs.
- CORE KITCHEN: full-service restaurant if available in the installed build; otherwise a clearly labeled food-court alternative.
- PATCH BAY: first aid / guest services.
- SERVICE MODE: staff and maintenance facilities.

Shell kit: charcoal wall panels, navy metal, red entry frames, electric-blue trim, pure-white canopies and RT sign panels. Use different massing and heights while keeping a common family; avoid repeating the exact same generic shop box everywhere.

## Theme music: Mombasa

Working interpretation is Hans Zimmer's Mombasa from Inception (2010). User has not supplied an audio file. No song was downloaded or purchased in this session. PC2 supports local custom audio; stage only a lawfully obtained local copy. Do not rip a stream, bypass DRM, upload the recording to this public repo, or embed it in a distributed park package. Speakers should cover the entrance and central show plaza in controlled zones, not play the same full-volume track on every ride. Add a music-off setting for recording/sharing.

## Construction order and acceptance

1. Preserve latest park and D: hash-matched backup before terrain/theming changes.
2. Establish one functional native test block: paths, queue, ride, food, drink, toilets, staff. OPEN in the UI is insufficient; observe guests reaching, boarding and leaving.
3. Native logo billboards: load originals from UserMedia, place gate/plaza/kiosk sign, save/reload and verify image persists.
4. Replace guest-visible grass with dark terrain/paving; strip tropical decorative shells; white lights first, red/blue accents second.
5. Build reusable branded facility shells and a native scenery van prototype; custom GLB import remains optional and unverified.
6. Reserve major coaster footprints and skyline corridors. Build/retest one flagship at a time and validate all special track operations.
7. Park acceptance requires connected entrances and exits, completed test runs, real guests riding, complete facilities, no visible grass inside themed areas, correct colors, three matching vans, and checkpoint reload.
